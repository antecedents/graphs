If a time series signal $\mathbf{y}$ has plausible seasonal & trend-cycle components, helpful/actionable observations can be garnered from their plausible decompositions1. In this context, an attendance numbers series $\mathbf{y}$ is $$\mathbf{y} = S + T + R$$ wherein $S$, $T$, & $R$ are the season, trend, & residue components of the series, respectively. The residue might be due to data collection irregularities, rare catastrophic events, etc.; uncertain2.
