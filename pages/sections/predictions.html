The predictions. Each graph title is a hospital name. These predictions can be improved substantially by having (a) much more granular data, e.g., hourly data rather than weekly data, (b) data segmented by emergency incident type, e.g., alcohol poisoning, road accidents, etc., (c) data segmented by age groups, (d) and much more. The advantage of a Bayesian approach - the results hint at the degree of uncertainty; the error bands and forecast bands suggests high uncertainty. Transparency forces us to act, e.g., collect much more granular data.
